Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026 · Operator: Igor Odariuk, odariuk.com

Short version

CanniScope runs in your browser. Your Search Console data — clicks, impressions, queries, positions — is processed on your device and is never sent to or stored on our servers. The only thing that leaves your browser is the list of page addresses the scan flagged, so we can check them on your live site.

Files you upload

CSV files you drop into CanniScope are read by your browser. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Signing in with Google

  • If you choose “Sign in with Google”, Google asks you to allow read-only access to Search Console (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly).
  • With that permission your browser lists your Search Console properties and downloads search performance data (queries, pages, clicks, impressions, positions) for the property you pick, directly from Google.
  • The access token stays in your browser's memory for the session. We never receive it, never store it, and it disappears when you close or reload the page. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • CanniScope cannot change anything in your Search Console account: the permission is read-only.

CanniScope's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to show you the cannibalization report you asked for. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone and not used to train AI models.

Live check of flagged pages

When the live check is on (you can turn it off before a scan), the addresses of flagged pages are sent to canniscope.odariuk.com/api/check. The checker opens those public pages the same way a search engine does and returns the status code, redirect target, canonical tag and noindex flag. Only page addresses are sent — no metrics, no queries. Addresses are not stored after the response; our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard short-term request logs.

Cookies and tracking

CanniScope sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers. No third-party fonts or scripts are loaded, except Google’s own sign-in script when you choose to sign in with Google.
